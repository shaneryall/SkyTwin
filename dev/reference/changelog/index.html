    Changelog¶

    All notable changes to Skytwin are listed here. The format follows Keep a Changelog, and versions follow Semantic Versioning.

    Unreleased¶

    0.1.0 - 2026-11-25¶

    Added¶

    • skytwin --version prints the version, for bug reports.
    • Training labels: every star, streak and target in the truth carries a label with its drawn path, matched-filter and peak signal-to-noise ratios against the frame's noise, extent above the noise and saturation. Rendered pixels are unchanged.
    • skytwin-export writes YOLO, YOLO-OBB and COCO training sets with tiling, a signal-to-noise threshold and a noise-normalised asinh stretch; skytwin.labels holds the conversions, and skytwin.torch gains detection_target and collate for batching frames with different numbers of objects. See docs/labels.md.
    • examples/track-rate.json: tracking a GEO satellite at its rate from a 790 km orbit through the example profile, so the stars trail into streaks and the satellite stays a point.
    • Rendering: point stars with a pixel-integrated Gaussian PSF, satellite streaks, FITS output (u16, i16, i32 or f32) and a truth file for every frame.
    • Sky: UCAC4 and CSV (Gaia) catalogues with proper motion and parallax; TAN and TAN-SIP WCS; stellar aberration and solar light deflection; passbands with AB zeropoints and colour terms.
    • Time and orbits: UTC/TT with leap seconds; observers and targets from TLEs (SGP4), state vectors or ephemeris tables; Earth shadow and occultation; Lambertian and faceted targets with glints and tumbling; Earthshine and Moonshine; target tracking.
    • Instrument profiles: processing, read-out noise, serial lag, fixed and smooth background fields, per-frame sky light, temperature and dark current, hot and flickering pixels, radiation hits with a rate map, CTI, PSF wings, Moffat tail and field variation, smear, bleeding, serial overflow, flat field with vignetting, PRNU, dust and field stop, radial lens distortion, pointing motion and header pointing errors, stray light, zodiacal light, chip layout and instrument-style headers, read-out faults.
    • Curved drift and image roll during the exposure (pointing.drift_turn_deg_per_s, roll_rate_deg_per_s, roll_centre_px): stars trail in arcs, and the reported roll follows the image.
    • Profiles as contracts: schema_version, sensor, learned provenance and the catalog a profile was calibrated against; scenes give only the catalogue's path.
    • Sequences, only_visible target filtering, and batches for training sets on all cores; per-pixel random streams for exact clean/dirty pairs.
    • Truth: Cramér–Rao bounds, trail ends, light curves, photon counts.
    • A provenance manifest for every run.
    • The skytwin Python package streaming frames to numpy and PyTorch.
    • skytwin-calibrate: learns a profile from real frames (headers, noise, photon-transfer gain, serial lag, hot pixels, flat and field stop, PSF, astrometry and distortion from the frames' own WCS, forced-photometry zeropoint and colour term, radiation, stray light, read-out faults), refines it against simulated twins, reports realism, scores detectors, and checks a catalogue against a profile.
    • learn --calibration reads the camera's bias, dark and flat frames for gain, read noise, dark current, radiation rate, read-out time and dust shadows; learn also reads the pixel size from the headers (XPIXSZ and kin).
    • learn handles frames whose stars trail, as when the telescope tracks a satellite: it measures each frame's trail and fits the PSF, zeropoint and radiation around the trails.
    • learn gives ground frames a zeropoint above the atmosphere, fitted against airmass, or moved with --extinction (default 0.2 mag/airmass) when the frames span too little airmass.
    • learn --ephemeris gives spacecraft states and sub-satellite points to frames whose headers carry none, from a CSV or a JPL Horizons vector table.
    • Profiles learned from public archives: profiles/cassiope-fai-nir.json (the DUST dataset), profiles/wise-w1.json (WISE's 3.4 µm band), profiles/astrianet-pl16803.json (a ground camera tracking satellites) and profiles/ckoirama-cdk24.json (a ground 0.6 m telescope). profiles/README.md gives each one's source and how closely its twins match.
    • A documentation site built from docs/, versioned with mike, with the command-line reference from --help and the Python API from docstrings.
    • Ground-site observers: a telescope at a WGS84 latitude, longitude and height, turning with the Earth (UT1 = UTC + DUT1), seen through the atmosphere (refraction from pressure and temperature, extinction per airmass, the horizon as the occulting edge); SITELAT, SITELONG, SITEELEV and AIRMASS header keywords and the site and atmosphere in the truth. Example examples/ground-tracks-meo.json.
